A Passion for Railroads

Our Mission

Engaging the community through model railroading, the Tennessee Valley Model Railroaders bring together families, hobbyists, and newcomers to celebrate craftsmanship, engineering, and storytelling on the rails.

Who We Are

The Tennessee Valley Model Railroad is a vibrant club dedicated to sharing the joy of model railroading. Open every Saturday, we offer free admission to come check out the newest additions and learn about this fun and exciting hobby. We aim to inspire the next generation to embrace this enriching hobby, providing resources and activities that enhance their experiences and ignite their passion.
